Product Description

A light day moisturizing cream suitable for all skin types. Contains natural ingredients that help moisturize the skin and protect it from external factors. Absorbs quickly and leaves no greasy residue on the skin.

Ideal for daily use before applying makeup or alone for radiant, moisturized skin all day long.

Contains a unique blend of natural ingredients that work together to provide deep, long-lasting hydration to the skin. It also helps improve skin elasticity and reduce the appearance of fine lines.

Ingredients

Water, Glycerin, Shea Butter, Jojoba Oil, Vitamin E, Sweet Almond Oil, Aloe Vera Extract, Green Tea Extract, Hyaluronic Acid, Argan Oil, Cucumber Extract, Pomegranate Extract.

All ingredients are natural and free from parabens, sulfates, and artificial fragrances.

How to Use

  1. Cleanse your skin thoroughly using a cleanser suitable for your skin type.
  2. Apply an appropriate amount of toner if you use one.
  3. Apply a small amount of cream to your fingertips.
  4. Gently spread the cream over the face and neck in circular motions.
  5. Allow the cream to fully absorb before applying makeup.

For best results, use the cream in the morning after cleansing the skin.
